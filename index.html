Our platform

Our main product is a booking system for organisations that schedule people on jobs, with a mobile app for the people doing the work.

The booking system

Create and manage bookings

Record each booking with its site, dates, roles and requirements, and see everything in one place.

Assign and confirm

Assign people to bookings and have them confirm shifts in the app, so you know who is turning up.

Timesheets and sign-off

Hours are recorded against each booking and approved with a secure signature, with a clear record of what was signed and when.

Invoicing

Approved timesheets feed into your accounting software so invoices are prepared accurately and on time.

The mobile app

Jobs and shifts

A simple list of upcoming jobs with the site, time and details, and one tap to confirm.

Check-in by location

Check in when you arrive on site, with the location recorded for an accurate start time.

Timesheets on the phone

Fill in the day, collect a signature on the screen and submit, with drafts saved if the connection drops.

Invoices and onboarding

View invoices and complete sign-up and onboarding from the same app, secured with a PIN.
